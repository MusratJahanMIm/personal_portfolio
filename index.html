Process

How I design

A simple, repeatable approach that keeps the work grounded in real user needs — not just aesthetics.

01

Discover

Interviews, competitor scans and context to understand the real problem.

02

Define

Turn insights into clear goals, user flows and success criteria.

03

Ideate

Sketch, wireframe and explore multiple directions before committing.

04

Prototype

High-fidelity screens and clickable prototypes in Figma.

05

Test

Quick usability checks with real people, then iterate fast.

06

Clean handoff, developer-friendly specs and continuous polish.

Principles

How I think about design

  • Clarity over cleverness — if it needs explaining, it's not finished.
  • Every pixel earns its place. No decoration without purpose.
  • Test with real people, not opinions in a meeting room.
  • Design with constraints — real users have slow phones and tired eyes.
  • Good design is invisible. Great design makes you feel capable.
  • Ship, learn, refine. Perfection is a moving target.
